<span class="model-tag">[四象－時間／日尺度｜旦慧晝安夕加夜甚]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：夫百病之所始生者，必起於<span class="c-metal">燥</span><span class="c-wood">溫</span><span class="c-water">寒</span><span class="c-fire">暑</span><span class="c-wood">風</span>雨<span class="c-neutral">陰陽</span>喜怒飲食居處，氣合而有形，得臟而有名，余知其然也。夫百病者，多以<span class="c-wood">旦慧</span><span class="c-fire">晝安</span>，<span class="c-metal">夕加</span><span class="c-water">夜甚</span>，何也？岐伯曰：四時之氣使然。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[四象－時間／日尺度｜朝春・日中夏・日入秋・夜半冬]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：願聞四時之氣。岐伯曰：<span class="c-wood">春生</span>，<span class="c-fire">夏長</span>，<span class="c-metal">秋收</span>，<span class="c-water">冬藏</span>，是氣之常也，人亦應之，以一日分為四時，<span class="c-wood">朝則為春</span>，<span class="c-fire">日中為夏</span>，<span class="c-metal">日入為秋</span>，<span class="c-water">夜半為冬</span>。<span class="c-wood">朝則人氣始生，病氣衰，故旦慧</span>；<span class="c-fire">日中人氣長，長則勝邪，故安</span>；<span class="c-metal">夕則人氣始衰，邪氣始生，故加</span>；<span class="c-water">夜半人氣入臟，邪氣獨居於身，故甚也</span>。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－時間／日尺度｜臟氣勝負決定反常時甚起]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：有時有反者何也？岐伯曰：是不應<span class="c-neutral">四時</span>之氣，<span class="c-yin">臟</span>獨主其病者，是必以<span class="c-yin">臟</span>氣之<span class="c-neutral">所不勝時者甚</span>，以其<span class="c-neutral">所勝時者起</span>也。黃帝曰：治之奈何？岐伯曰：順天之時，而病可與期。順者為工，逆者為麤。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜五臟五變五輸二十五]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：善，余聞刺有五變，以主五輸。願聞其數。岐伯曰：人有<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>，<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>有五變。五變有五輸，故<span class="c-neutral">五五二十五輸</span>，以應五時。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－時間／十日尺度｜五臟・五色・五時・五音・五味・天干]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-earth">黃</span>帝曰：願聞五變。岐伯曰：<span class="c-wood">肝</span>為牡藏，其色<span class="c-wood">青</span>，其時<span class="c-wood">春</span>，其音<span class="c-wood">角</span>，其味<span class="c-wood">酸</span>，其日<span class="c-wood">甲乙</span>；<span class="c-fire">心</span>為牡藏，其色<span class="c-fire">赤</span>，其時<span class="c-fire">夏</span>，其日<span class="c-fire">丙丁</span>，其音<span class="c-fire">徵</span>，其味<span class="c-fire">苦</span>；<span class="c-earth">脾</span>為牝藏，其色<span class="c-earth">黃</span>，其時<span class="c-earth">長夏</span>，其日<span class="c-earth">戊己</span>，其音<span class="c-earth">宮</span>，其味<span class="c-earth">甘</span>；<span class="c-metal">肺</span>為牝藏，其色<span class="c-metal">白</span>，其音<span class="c-metal">商</span>，其時<span class="c-fire">徵</span>，其日<span class="c-metal">庚辛</span>，其味<span class="c-metal">辛</span>；<span class="c-water">腎</span>為牝藏，其色<span class="c-water">黑</span>，其時<span class="c-water">冬</span>，其日<span class="c-water">壬癸</span>，其音<span class="c-water">羽</span>，其味<span class="c-water">咸</span>。是為五變。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－時間／年尺度｜五時→五輸]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：以主五<span class="c-fire">輸</span>奈何？藏主<span class="c-water">冬</span>，<span class="c-water">冬</span>刺<span class="c-water">井</span>；色主<span class="c-wood">春</span>，<span class="c-wood">春</span>刺<span class="c-wood">滎</span>；時主<span class="c-fire">夏</span>，<span class="c-fire">夏</span>刺<span class="c-fire">輸</span>；音主<span class="c-earth">長夏</span>，<span class="c-earth">長夏</span>刺<span class="c-earth">經</span>；味主<span class="c-metal">秋</span>，<span class="c-metal">秋</span>刺<span class="c-metal">合</span>。是謂五變，以主五<span class="c-fire">輸</span>。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－五輸｜原不應五時]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：諸<span class="c-neutral">原</span>安和，以致五輸。岐伯曰：<span class="c-neutral">原</span>獨不應<span class="c-neutral">五時</span>，以經合之，以應其數，故六六三十六輸。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－五輸｜病變類型→井滎輸經合]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：何謂藏主冬，時主夏，音主長夏，味主秋，色主春。願聞其故。岐伯曰：<span class="c-water">病在藏者</span>，取之<span class="c-water">井</span>；<span class="c-wood">病變於色者</span>，取之<span class="c-wood">滎</span>；<span class="c-fire">病時間時甚者</span>，取之<span class="c-fire">輸</span>；<span class="c-earth">病變於音者</span>，取之<span class="c-earth">經</span>；<span class="c-metal">經滿而血者，病在胃；及以飲食不節得病者</span>，取之於<span class="c-metal">合</span>，故命曰味主<span class="c-metal">合</span>。是謂五變也。</div>
