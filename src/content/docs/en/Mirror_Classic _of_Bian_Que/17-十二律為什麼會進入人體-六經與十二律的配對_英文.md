In the “Sixty Shou,” the *Mirror Classic of Bian Que* gives a crucial pairing table:

> Huangzhong and Nanlü are Taiyin;
> Ruibin and Jiazhong are Taiyang;
> Taicu and Linzhong are Shaoyang;
> Yize and Dalü are Shaoyin;
> Guxian and Zhonglü are Yangming;
> Wushe and Yingzhong are Jueyin.

The Twelve Lü are thus divided into six pairs, two lü for each channel.

## Do not first interpret lü as musical pitch alone

In the first series, Huangzhong was already defined as the pulse-route baseline of “qi traveling six cun in one breath of a normal person.” Later, “Kuidu” gives different divisions for other lü, such as Dalü at fifty-seven and a half fen.

Within the *Mirror Classic*’s medical context, lü therefore carries at least the role of a **scale of pulse-and-breath movement**.

The pairing of the Twelve Lü with the Six Channels can thus first be understood as follows: **Six-Channel qi movement is placed into twelve different regulated-measure coordinates.**

This is more faithful to the text than claiming directly that a certain channel “equals” a certain modern frequency.

## Why two lü for each channel?

The passage does not explain this in modern terms, but it clearly divides the Twelve Lü evenly into six pairs.

More importantly, the same pairings recur in the final sections on human types: Shaoyin uses Dalü and Yize; Taiyin uses Huangzhong and Nanlü; Jueyin uses Yingzhong and Wushe; Shaoyang uses Taicu and Linzhong; Yangming uses Guxian and Zhonglü; Taiyang uses Jiazhong and Ruibin.

The table is therefore not a one-time symbolic correspondence. It is a foundational codebook reused in the later parts of the work.

## The Six Channels are organized into three pairs

The *Mirror Classic* also pairs the Six Channels into:

- Taiyin and Taiyang;
- Shaoyin and Shaoyang;
- Jueyin and Yangming.

It associates these respectively with “warp and weft,” “pivot and sustaining,” and “the four dimensions.”

The Twelve Lü are therefore not pasted onto isolated channel names; they enter a larger spatial structure.

## Lü turns the Six Channels from names into operable positions

If the Six Channels were only six disease categories, the Sixty Shou would have little room for finer differentiation. Once each channel is split across two lü, another phase or scale layer appears.

Adding transformation tones, ten degrees, and left-right positions then allows many distinct states within the same “Taiyin.”

This is characteristic of the *Mirror Classic*: **rather than asking one term to carry every phenomenon, it repeatedly decomposes state into combinable dimensions.**
