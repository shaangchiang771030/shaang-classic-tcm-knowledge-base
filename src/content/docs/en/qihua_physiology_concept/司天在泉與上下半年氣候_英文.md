The *Waijing Weiyan* states that the qi of Heaven and Earth are constantly interacting: Heavenly Administration (*si tian*) broadly governs the upper region, while Terrestrial Effect (*zai quan*) broadly governs the lower, but the two should not be cut apart as completely separate halves.

This version is more consistent with a continuous model of Heaven–Earth interaction. It also provides the rationale, within this framework, for why the flavors or properties of substances arising from Earth may vary according to the annual *zai quan* condition.

A close reading of disorders associated with *si tian* and *zai quan* in the *Zhi Zhen Yao Da Lun* suggests another pattern: *si tian* disorders tend to concentrate above the navel, treated here as the boundary of Heaven–Earth qi-interchange; *zai quan* disorders tend to concentrate in the lower burner and the limbs, or may reach the upper burner when qi surges upward.

The following summary from the *Waijing Weiyan* may be used as a reference:

> **Treating disorders of Heavenly Administration:** pathogenic influence is received from above; disease is more often seen in the Heart and Lung, the channels and collaterals, and the qi level.
>
> **Treating disorders of Terrestrial Effect:** pathogenic influence is received from below; disease is more often seen in the Liver and Kidney, the uterus/reproductive network, and the blood level.
