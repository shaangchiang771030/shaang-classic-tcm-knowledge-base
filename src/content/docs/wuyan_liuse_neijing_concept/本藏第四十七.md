<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體｜五臟陰・六腑陽]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜筋骨皮肉腠理]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝問於岐伯曰：人之血氣精神者，所以奉生而周於性命者也；經脈者，所以行血氣而營<span class="c-neutral">陰陽</span>、濡<span class="c-neutral">筋骨</span>，利關節者也；衛氣者，所以<span class="c-wood">溫</span><span class="c-earth">分肉</span>，充<span class="c-metal">皮膚</span>，肥<span class="c-metal">腠理</span>，司開闔者也；志意者，所以御精神，收魂魄，適<span class="c-water">寒</span><span class="c-wood">溫</span>，和喜怒者也。是故血和則經脈流行，營復<span class="c-neutral">陰陽</span>，<span class="c-neutral">筋骨</span>勁強，關節清利矣；衛氣和則<span class="c-earth">分肉</span>解利，<span class="c-metal">皮膚</span>調柔，<span class="c-metal">腠理</span>致密矣；志意和則精神專直，魂魄不散，悔怒不起，<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>不受邪矣；<span class="c-water">寒</span><span class="c-wood">溫</span>和則<span class="c-yang">六腑</span>化谷，<span class="c-wood">風</span>痹不作，經脈通利，肢節得安矣，此人之常平也。<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>者，所以藏精神血氣魂魄者也；<span class="c-yang">六腑</span>者，所以化水谷而行津液者也。此人之所以具受於天也，無愚智賢不肖，無以相倚也。然有其獨盡天壽，而無邪僻之病，百年不衰，雖犯<span class="c-wood">風</span>雨卒<span class="c-water">寒</span>大<span class="c-fire">暑</span>，猶有弗能害也；有其不離屏蔽室內，無怵惕之恐，然猶不免於病，何也？願聞其故。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽／時間｜五臟參天地、副陰陽、運四時]</span>
<div class="model-map">岐伯對曰：窘乎哉問也。<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>者，所以參<span class="c-neutral">天地</span>，副<span class="c-neutral">陰陽</span>，而運<span class="c-neutral">四時</span>，化五節者也；<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>者，固有小大、高下、堅脆、端正、偏傾者，<span class="c-yang">六腑</span>亦有小大、長短、厚薄、結直、緩急。凡此二十五者，各不同，或善或惡，或吉或凶，請言其方。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜心形質二十五變]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-fire">心</span>小則<span class="c-fire">安，邪弗能傷，易傷以憂</span>；<span class="c-fire">心</span>大則<span class="c-fire">憂，不能傷，易傷於邪</span>。<span class="c-fire">心</span>高則<span class="c-fire">滿於肺中，悗而善忘，難開以言</span>；<span class="c-fire">心</span>下，則<span class="c-fire">藏外，易傷於寒，易恐以言</span>。<span class="c-fire">心</span>堅，則<span class="c-fire">藏安守固</span>；<span class="c-fire">心</span>脆則<span class="c-fire">善病消癉熱中</span>。<span class="c-fire">心</span>端正，則<span class="c-fire">和利難傷</span>；<span class="c-fire">心</span>偏傾則<span class="c-fire">操持不一，無守司也</span>。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜肺形質二十五變]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-metal">肺</span>小，則<span class="c-metal">少飲，不病喘喝</span>；<span class="c-metal">肺</span>大則<span class="c-metal">多飲，善病胸痹、喉痹、逆氣</span>。<span class="c-metal">肺</span>高，則<span class="c-metal">上氣，肩息咳</span>；<span class="c-metal">肺</span>下則居賁迫<span class="c-metal">肺</span>，<span class="c-metal">善脅下痛</span>。<span class="c-metal">肺</span>堅則<span class="c-metal">不病，咳上氣</span>；<span class="c-metal">肺</span>脆，則<span class="c-metal">苦病消痹易傷</span>。<span class="c-metal">肺</span>端正，則<span class="c-metal">和利難傷</span>；<span class="c-metal">肺</span>偏傾，則<span class="c-metal">胸偏痛也</span>。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜肝形質二十五變]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-wood">肝</span>小則<span class="c-wood">臟安，無脅下之病</span>；<span class="c-wood">肝</span>大則逼胃迫咽，迫咽則<span class="c-wood">苦膈中，且脅下痛</span>。<span class="c-wood">肝</span>高，則<span class="c-wood">上支賁切，脅輓為息賁</span>；<span class="c-wood">肝</span>下則逼胃脅下空，<span class="c-wood">脅下空則易受邪</span>。<span class="c-wood">肝</span>堅則<span class="c-wood">藏安難傷</span>；<span class="c-wood">肝</span>脆則<span class="c-wood">善病消痹，易傷</span>。<span class="c-wood">肝</span>端正，則<span class="c-wood">和利難傷</span>；<span class="c-wood">肝</span>偏傾，則<span class="c-wood">脅下痛也</span>。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜脾形質二十五變]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-earth">脾</span>小，則<span class="c-earth">臟安，難傷於邪也</span>；<span class="c-earth">脾</span>大，則<span class="c-earth">苦湊（月少）而痛，不能疾行</span>。<span class="c-earth">脾</span>高，則<span class="c-earth">（月少）引季脅而痛</span>；<span class="c-earth">脾</span>下則下歸於大腸，下加於大腸，則<span class="c-earth">臟苦受邪</span>。<span class="c-earth">脾</span>堅，則<span class="c-earth">臟安難傷</span>；<span class="c-earth">脾</span>脆，則<span class="c-earth">善病消痹易傷</span>。<span class="c-earth">脾</span>端正，則<span class="c-earth">和利難傷</span>；<span class="c-earth">脾</span>偏傾，則<span class="c-earth">善滿善脹也</span>。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜腎形質二十五變]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-water">腎</span>小，則<span class="c-water">臟安難傷</span>；<span class="c-water">腎</span>大，則<span class="c-water">善病腰痛，不可以俛仰，易傷以邪</span>。<span class="c-water">腎</span>高，則<span class="c-water">苦背膂痛，不可以俛仰</span>；<span class="c-water">腎</span>下則<span class="c-water">腰尻痛，不可以俛仰，為狐疝</span>。<span class="c-water">腎</span>堅，則<span class="c-water">不病腰背痛</span>；<span class="c-water">腎</span>脆，則<span class="c-water">善病消癉，易傷</span>。<span class="c-water">腎</span>端正，則<span class="c-water">和利難傷</span>；<span class="c-water">腎</span>偏傾，則<span class="c-water">苦腰尻痛也</span>。凡此二十五變者，人之所苦常病。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜赤色外候心]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：何以知其然也？岐伯曰：<span class="c-fire">赤色</span>小理者，<span class="c-fire">心</span>小；麤理者，<span class="c-fire">心</span>大。無（骨曷）（骨虧）者，<span class="c-fire">心</span>高；（骨曷）（骨虧）小、短、舉者，<span class="c-fire">心</span>下。（骨曷）（骨虧）長者，<span class="c-fire">心</span>下堅；（骨曷）（骨虧）弱小以薄者，<span class="c-fire">心</span>脆。（骨曷）（骨虧）直下不舉者，<span class="c-fire">心</span>端正；（骨曷）（骨虧）倚一方者，<span class="c-fire">心</span>偏傾也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜白色外候肺]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-metal">白色</span>小理者，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>小；麤理者，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>大。巨肩反膺陷喉者，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>高；合腋張脅者，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>下。好肩背厚者，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>堅；肩背薄者，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>脆。背膺厚者，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>端正；脅偏疏者，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>偏傾也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜青色外候肝]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-wood">青色</span>小理者，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>小；麤理者，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>大。廣胸反骹者，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>高；合脅兔骹者，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>下。胸脅好者，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>堅；脅骨弱者，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>脆。膺腹好相得者，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>端正；脅骨偏舉者，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>偏傾也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜黃色外候脾]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-earth">黃色</span>小理者，<span class="c-earth">脾</span>小；麤理者，<span class="c-earth">脾</span>大。揭唇者，<span class="c-earth">脾</span>高；唇下縱者，<span class="c-earth">脾</span>下。唇堅者，<span class="c-earth">脾</span>堅；唇大而不堅者，<span class="c-earth">脾</span>脆。唇上下好者，<span class="c-earth">脾</span>端正；唇偏舉者，<span class="c-earth">脾</span>偏傾也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜黑色外候腎]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-water">黑色</span>小理者，<span class="c-water">腎</span>小；麤理者，<span class="c-water">腎</span>大。高耳者，<span class="c-water">腎</span>高；耳後陷者，<span class="c-water">腎</span>下。耳堅者，<span class="c-water">腎</span>堅；耳薄而不堅者，<span class="c-water">腎</span>脆。耳好前居牙車者，<span class="c-water">腎</span>端正；耳偏高者，<span class="c-water">腎</span>偏傾也。凡此諸變者，持則安，減則病也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體｜五臟六腑為邪捨]</span>
<div class="model-map">帝曰：善。然非余之所問也，願聞人之有不可病者，至盡天壽，雖有深擾大恐，怵惕之志，猶不能減也，甚寒大熱，不能傷也；其有不離屏蔽室內，又無怵惕之恐，然不免於病者，何也？願聞其故。岐伯曰：<span class="c-yin">五臟</span><span class="c-yang">六腑</span>，邪之捨也，請言其故。<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆小者，少病，苦憔心，大愁擾；<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆大者，緩於事，難使以擾。<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆高者，好高舉措；<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆下者，好出人下。<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆堅者，無病；<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆脆者，不離於病。<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆端正者，和利得人心；<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆偏傾者，邪心而善盜，不可以為人平，反復言語也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜五臟合六腑及外應]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：願聞六腑之應。岐伯答曰：<span class="c-metal">肺</span>合<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>，<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>者，<span class="c-metal">皮</span>其應；<span class="c-fire">心</span>合<span class="c-fire">小腸</span>，<span class="c-fire">小腸</span>者，<span class="c-fire">脈</span>其應；<span class="c-wood">肝</span>合<span class="c-wood">膽</span>，<span class="c-wood">膽</span>者，<span class="c-wood">筋</span>其應；<span class="c-earth">脾</span>合<span class="c-earth">胃</span>，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>者，<span class="c-earth">肉</span>其應；<span class="c-water">腎</span>合<span class="c-fire">三焦</span><span class="c-water">膀胱</span>，<span class="c-fire">三焦</span><span class="c-water">膀胱</span>者，腠理毫毛其應。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜肺－皮－大腸外內相應]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：應之奈何？岐伯曰：<span class="c-metal">肺</span>應<span class="c-metal">皮</span>。<span class="c-metal">皮</span>厚者，<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>厚，<span class="c-metal">皮</span>薄者，<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>薄；<span class="c-metal">皮</span>緩，腹里大者，<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>大而長；<span class="c-metal">皮</span>急者，<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>急而短；<span class="c-metal">皮</span>滑者，<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>直；<span class="c-metal">皮</span>肉不相離者，<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>結。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜心－脈－小腸外內相應]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-fire">心</span>應<span class="c-fire">脈</span>，皮厚者，<span class="c-fire">脈</span>厚，<span class="c-fire">脈</span>厚者，<span class="c-fire">小腸</span>厚；皮薄者，<span class="c-fire">脈</span>薄，<span class="c-fire">脈</span>薄者，<span class="c-fire">小腸</span>薄；皮緩者，<span class="c-fire">脈</span>緩，<span class="c-fire">脈</span>緩者，<span class="c-fire">小腸</span>大而長；皮薄而<span class="c-fire">脈</span>衝小者，<span class="c-fire">小腸</span>小而短。諸<span class="c-yang">陽經脈</span>皆多紆屈者，<span class="c-fire">小腸</span>結。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜脾－肉－胃外內相應]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-earth">脾</span>應<span class="c-earth">肉</span>，<span class="c-earth">肉</span>（月囷）堅大者，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>厚；<span class="c-earth">肉</span>（月囷）幺者，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>薄。<span class="c-earth">肉</span>（月囷）小而幺者，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>不堅；<span class="c-earth">肉</span>（月囷）不稱身者，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>下，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>下者，下管約不利。<span class="c-earth">肉</span>（月囷）不堅者，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>緩；<span class="c-earth">肉</span>（月囷）無小里累者，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>急。<span class="c-earth">肉</span>（月囷）多少里累者，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>結，<span class="c-earth">胃</span>結者，上管約不利也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜肝－爪－膽外內相應]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-wood">肝</span>應爪，爪厚<span class="c-earth">色黃</span>者，<span class="c-wood">膽</span>厚；爪薄<span class="c-fire">色紅</span>者，<span class="c-wood">膽</span>薄；爪堅<span class="c-wood">色青</span>者，<span class="c-wood">膽</span>急；爪濡<span class="c-fire">色赤</span>者，<span class="c-wood">膽</span>緩；爪直<span class="c-metal">色白</span>無約者，<span class="c-wood">膽</span>直；爪惡<span class="c-water">色黑</span>多紋者，<span class="c-wood">膽</span>結也。</div>
