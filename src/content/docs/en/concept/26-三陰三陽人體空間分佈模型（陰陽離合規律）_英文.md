The Three Yin and Three Yang are not only used to organize interlocking time axes such as host qi, guest qi, daily governance, and desired relief times. They are also presented as an invisible but ordered spatial grid in the anatomy and geometry of the human body. This is the **human spatial distribution model of the Three Yin and Three Yang**, one of the central spatial models in channel theory.
The core hypothesis is a **topological and geometric blueprint of biological evolution under the influence of cosmic energy fields**. In this proposal, the spatial distribution of the channels is not random. Rather, during mammalian evolution, a three-dimensional energetic antenna network is said to have developed in a way that maximizes resonance and exchange with larger environmental fields. Its distribution follows the classical **law of separation and union of Yin and Yang**, expressed as **opening, closing, and pivoting (kai, he, shu)**.
