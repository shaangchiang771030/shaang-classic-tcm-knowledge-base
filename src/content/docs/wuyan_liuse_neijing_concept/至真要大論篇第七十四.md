黃帝問曰：五氣交合，盈虛更作，余知之矣。六氣分治，司天地者，其至何如？  

岐伯再拜對曰：明乎哉問也。天地之大紀，人神之通應也。  

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／天地]</span>

帝曰：願聞<span class="c-yang">上</span>合昭昭，<span class="c-yin">下</span>合冥冥奈何？  

岐伯曰：此道之所主，工之所疑也。  

帝曰：願聞其道也。  

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－時間／地支每六年]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[六淫－年尺度]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>司天，其化以<span class="c-jueyin">風</span>；<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>司天，其化以<span class="c-hot">熱</span>；<span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>司天，其化以<span class="c-taiyin">濕</span>；<span class="c-shaoyang">少陽</span>司天，其化以<span class="c-shaoyang">火</span>；<span class="c-yangming">陽明</span>司天，其化以<span class="c-yangming">燥</span>；<span class="c-taiyang">太陽</span>司天，其化以<span class="c-cold">寒</span>，以所臨臟位，命其病者也。  
</div>

帝曰：地化奈何？  

岐伯曰：司天同候，間氣皆然。  

帝曰：間氣何謂？  

岐伯曰：司左右者是謂間氣也。  

帝曰：何以異之？  

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－時間／年尺度｜主歲・間氣]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：主歲者紀歲，間氣者紀步也。  
</div>

帝曰：善。歲主奈何？  

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－時間／地支每六年｜司天・在泉]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／五味・五色]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>司天為<span class="c-jueyin">風化</span>，在泉為<span class="c-wood">酸化</span>，司氣為<span class="c-wood">蒼化</span>，間氣為<span class="c-jueyin">動化</span>。  

<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>司天為<span class="c-shaoyin">熱化</span>，在泉為<span class="c-fire">苦化</span>，不司氣化，居氣為<span class="c-shaoyin">灼化</span>。  

<span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>司天為<span class="c-taiyin">濕化</span>，在泉為<span class="c-earth">甘化</span>，司氣為<span class="c-earth">黅化</span>，間氣為<span class="c-taiyin">柔化</span>。  

<span class="c-shaoyang">少陽</span>司天為<span class="c-shaoyang">火化</span>，在泉為<span class="c-fire">苦化</span>，司氣為<span class="c-fire">丹化</span>，間氣為<span class="c-shaoyang">明化</span>。  

<span class="c-yangming">陽明</span>司天為<span class="c-yangming">燥化</span>，在泉為<span class="c-metal">辛化</span>，司氣為<span class="c-metal">素化</span>，間氣為<span class="c-yangming">清化</span>。  

<span class="c-taiyang">太陽</span>司天為<span class="c-taiyang">寒化</span>，在泉為<span class="c-water">咸化</span>，司氣為<span class="c-water">玄化</span>，間氣為<span class="c-taiyang">藏化</span>。  
</div>

故治病者，必明六化分治，五味五色所生，五藏所宜，乃可以言盈虛病生之緒也。  

帝曰：<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>在泉，而<span class="c-wood">酸化</span>先，余知之矣。<span class="c-jueyin">風化</span>之行也何如？  

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／天地]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－時間／年尺度｜六節]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：<span class="c-jueyin">風行於地</span>，所謂本也，余氣同法。本乎<span class="c-yang">天</span>者，<span class="c-yang">天</span>之氣也；本乎<span class="c-yin">地</span>者，<span class="c-yin">地</span>之氣也。<span class="c-yang">天</span><span class="c-yin">地</span>合氣，六節分而萬物化生矣。故曰：謹候氣宜，無失病機，此之謂也。  
</div>

帝曰：其主病何如？  

岐伯曰：司歲備物，則無遺主矣。  

帝曰：先歲物何也？  

岐伯曰：天地之專精也。  

帝曰：司氣者何如？  

岐伯曰：司氣者主歲同然，有餘不足也。  

帝曰：非司歲物何謂也？  

岐伯曰：散也，故質同而升等也。氣味有薄厚，性用有躁靜，治保有多少，力化有淺深，此之謂也。  

帝曰：歲主臟害何謂？  

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間（相克）]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：以所不勝命之，則其要也。  

帝曰：治之奈何？  

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體]</span>

岐伯曰：<span class="c-yang">上</span>淫於<span class="c-yin">下</span>，所勝平之；<span class="c-yang">外</span>淫於<span class="c-yin">內</span>，所勝治之。  
</div>

帝曰：善。平氣何如？  

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：謹察<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>所在而調之，以平為期。正者正治，反者反治。  

帝曰：夫子言察<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>所在而調之，論言人迎與寸口相應，若引繩，小大齊等，命曰平。<span class="c-yin">陰</span>之所在寸口，何如？  

岐伯曰：視歲南北可知之矣。  

帝曰：願卒聞之。  

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－時間／地支每六年｜南北政]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體]</span>

岐伯曰：北政之歲，<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>在泉，則寸口不應；<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>在泉，則右不應；<span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>在泉，則左不應；南政之歲，<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>司天，則寸口不應；<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>司天，則右不應；<span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>司天，則左不應；諸不應者反其診則見矣。  

帝曰：尺候何如？  

岐伯曰：北政之歲，<span class="c-yin">三陰</span>在<span class="c-yin">下</span>，則寸不應，<span class="c-yin">三陰</span>在<span class="c-yang">上</span>，則尺不應。南政之歲，<span class="c-yin">三陰</span>在<span class="c-yang">天</span>，則寸不應，<span class="c-yin">三陰</span>在泉，則尺不應，左右同。故曰知其要者，一言而終，不知其要，流散無窮，此之謂也。  
</div>

帝曰：善。天地之氣，內淫而病何如？  

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－時間／地支每六年｜在泉]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[六淫－年尺度]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間（相克）]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：歲<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>在泉，<span class="c-jueyin">風</span>淫所勝，則<span class="c-jueyin">地氣不明，平野昧，草乃早秀</span>。民病<span class="c-earth">灑灑振寒，善伸數欠</span>，<span class="c-wood">心痛支滿，兩脅里急</span>，<span class="c-earth">飲食不下，膈咽不通，食則嘔，腹脹善噫</span>，<span class="c-earth">得後與氣，則快然如衰</span>，<span class="c-earth">身體皆重</span>。  

歲<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>在泉，<span class="c-hot">熱</span>淫所勝，則<span class="c-shaoyin">焰浮川澤</span>，<span class="c-yin">陰處</span>反明。民病<span class="c-metal">腹中常嗚，氣上衝胸、喘、不能久立</span>，<span class="c-cold">寒</span><span class="c-hot">熱</span><span class="c-metal">皮膚痛、目瞑齒痛、（出頁）腫</span>、<span class="c-cold">惡寒</span><span class="c-hot">發熱</span><span class="c-metal">如瘧</span>，少腹中痛、腹大、<span class="c-hot">蟄蟲不藏</span>。  

歲<span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>在泉，<span class="c-taiyin">草乃早榮</span>，<span class="c-taiyin">濕</span>淫所勝，則<span class="c-taiyin">埃昏岩谷</span>，<span class="c-earth">黃</span>反見<span class="c-water">黑</span>，<span class="c-taiyin">至陰</span>之交。民病<span class="c-shaoyang">飲積心痛，耳聾，渾渾焞焞，嗌腫喉痹</span>，<span class="c-yin">陰病</span>血見，<span class="c-water">少腹痛腫，不得小便，病衝頭痛，目似脫，項似拔，腰似折，髀不可以回，膕如結，腨如別</span>。  

歲<span class="c-shaoyang">少陽</span>在泉，<span class="c-shaoyang">火</span>淫所勝，則<span class="c-shaoyang">焰明郊野</span>，<span class="c-cold">寒</span><span class="c-hot">熱</span>更至。民病<span class="c-hot">注洩赤白</span>，<span class="c-hot">少腹痛，溺赤</span>，甚則<span class="c-hot">血便</span>，<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>同候。  

歲<span class="c-yangming">陽明</span>在泉，<span class="c-yangming">燥</span>淫所勝，則<span class="c-yangming">霧霧清瞑</span>。民病<span class="c-wood">喜嘔，嘔有苦，善太息，心脅痛，不能反側</span>，甚則<span class="c-yangming">嗌乾，面塵，身無膏澤</span>，<span class="c-hot">足外反熱</span>。  

歲<span class="c-taiyang">太陽</span>在泉，<span class="c-cold">寒</span>淫所勝，則<span class="c-taiyang">凝肅慘栗</span>。民病<span class="c-fire">少腹控睪引腰脊，上衝心痛，血見嗌痛，頷腫</span>。  
</div>

帝曰：善。治之奈何？  

<span class="model-tag">[六淫－年尺度｜在泉治法]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[五行－五味]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：諸氣在泉，<span class="c-jueyin">風淫於內</span>，治以<span class="c-metal">辛</span><span class="c-cool">涼</span>，佐以<span class="c-fire">苦</span>；以<span class="c-earth">甘</span>緩之，以<span class="c-metal">辛</span>散之；<span class="c-shaoyin">熱淫於內</span>，治以<span class="c-water">咸</span><span class="c-cold">寒</span>，佐以<span class="c-earth">甘</span><span class="c-fire">苦</span>，以<span class="c-wood">酸</span>收之，以<span class="c-fire">苦</span>發之；<span class="c-taiyin">濕淫於內</span>，治以<span class="c-fire">苦</span><span class="c-hot">熱</span>，佐以<span class="c-wood">酸</span><span class="c-neutral">淡</span>，以<span class="c-fire">苦</span>燥之，以<span class="c-neutral">淡</span>洩之，<span class="c-shaoyang">火淫於內</span>，治以<span class="c-water">咸</span><span class="c-cold">冷</span>，佐以<span class="c-fire">苦</span><span class="c-metal">辛</span>，以<span class="c-wood">酸</span>收之，以<span class="c-fire">苦</span>發之；<span class="c-yangming">燥淫於內</span>，治以<span class="c-fire">苦</span><span class="c-warm">溫</span>，佐以<span class="c-earth">甘</span><span class="c-metal">辛</span>，以<span class="c-fire">苦</span>下之；<span class="c-taiyang">寒淫於內</span>，治以<span class="c-earth">甘</span><span class="c-hot">熱</span>，佐以<span class="c-fire">苦</span><span class="c-metal">辛</span>，以<span class="c-water">咸</span>瀉之，以<span class="c-metal">辛</span>潤之，以<span class="c-fire">苦</span>堅之。  
</div>

帝曰：善。天氣之變何如？  

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－時間／地支每六年｜司天]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[六淫－年尺度]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間（相克）]</span>

<div class="model-map">
岐伯曰：<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>司天，<span class="c-jueyin">風</span>淫所勝，則<span class="c-jueyin">太虛埃昏，雲物以擾</span>，<span class="c-cold">寒</span>生<span class="c-wood">春氣</span>，<span class="c-jueyin">流水不冰</span>。民病<span class="c-earth">胃脘當心而痛，上肢兩脅，膈咽不通，飲食不下，舌本強，食則嘔，</span><span class="c-cold">冷</span><span class="c-earth">洩腹脹，溏洩瘕水閉</span>，蟄蟲不去病本於<span class="c-earth">脾</span>。<span class="c-earth">衝陽</span>絕，死不治。  

<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>司天，<span class="c-hot">熱</span>淫所勝，<span class="c-shaoyin">怫熱至，火行其政</span>。民病<span class="c-metal">胸中煩</span><span class="c-hot">熱</span>，<span class="c-metal">嗌乾、右胠滿、皮膚痛</span>，<span class="c-cold">寒</span><span class="c-hot">熱</span><span class="c-metal">咳喘</span>，大雨且至、<span class="c-metal">唾血血洩、鼽衄、嚏嘔、溺色變</span>，甚則<span class="c-hot">瘡瘍</span><span class="c-metal">胕腫、肩背臂臑及缺盆中痛</span>，<span class="c-metal">心痛肺䐜，腹大滿，膨膨而喘咳</span>，病本於<span class="c-metal">肺</span>，<span class="c-metal">尺澤</span>絕，死不治。  

<span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>司天，<span class="c-taiyin">濕</span>淫所勝，則<span class="c-yin">沈陰</span>且布，<span class="c-taiyin">雨變枯槁</span>，<span class="c-water">胕腫骨痛，陰痹</span>。<span class="c-water">陰痹</span>者，按之不得，<span class="c-water">腰脊頭項痛、時眩</span>、<span class="c-water">大便難</span>，<span class="c-yin">陰氣</span>不用，<span class="c-water">飢不欲食</span>，<span class="c-water">咳唾則有血，心如懸</span>。病本於<span class="c-water">腎</span>，<span class="c-water">太溪</span>絕，死不治。
