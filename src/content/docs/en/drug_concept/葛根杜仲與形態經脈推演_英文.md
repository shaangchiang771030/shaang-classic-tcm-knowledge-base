Kudzu and eucommia display two different pathways of medicinal derivation: one emphasizes **morphology and upward reach**, while the other emphasizes **channels and the essence of Metal-Water**. Together they show how materia-medica interpretation can simultaneously use rules of form, body, channels, zang, and spirit.

Kudzu belongs to the vine category. Vines carry the image of spreading, pulling, and moving upward from below, so kudzu can be understood as lifting Yin qi, opening the Taiyang channels, and harmonizing Yangming qi. This gives a consistent direction to its use in releasing the muscles, raising fluids, and relieving alcohol effects.

Eucommia is summarized as a medicine related to Yangming and Shaoyin Metal-Water essence. Its associations with the waist, knees, sinews, bones, and strengthening the will can all be returned to *Inner Canon* rules involving Kidney, bone, will, and the Yangming ancestral sinews. When weakness and atrophy occur, entering through Yangming is therefore also an important line of reasoning.
