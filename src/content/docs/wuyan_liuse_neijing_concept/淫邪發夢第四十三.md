<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體｜外襲內・臟腑有餘不足]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：願聞淫邪泮衍，奈何？岐伯曰：正邪從<span class="c-yang">外</span>襲<span class="c-yin">內</span>，而未有定捨，反淫於<span class="c-yin">臟</span>，不得定處，與<span class="c-yin">營</span><span class="c-yang">衛</span>俱行，而與魂魄飛揚，使人臥不得安而喜夢；氣淫於<span class="c-yang">腑</span>，則有餘於<span class="c-yang">外</span>，不足於<span class="c-yin">內</span>；氣淫於<span class="c-yin">臟</span>，則有餘於<span class="c-yin">內</span>，不足於<span class="c-yang">外</span>。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體｜十二盛夢象]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜五臟氣盛夢象]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：有餘不足，有形乎？岐伯曰：<span class="c-yin">陰氣盛</span>，則夢涉<span class="c-water">大水</span>而恐懼；<span class="c-yang">陽氣盛</span>，則夢<span class="c-fire">大火</span>而燔?；<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>俱盛，則夢相殺。<span class="c-yang">上</span>盛則夢飛，<span class="c-yin">下</span>盛則夢墮；甚飢則夢取，甚飽則夢予；<span class="c-wood">肝氣盛</span>，則<span class="c-wood">夢怒</span>，<span class="c-metal">肺氣盛</span>，則<span class="c-metal">夢恐懼、哭泣、飛揚</span>；<span class="c-fire">心氣盛</span>，則<span class="c-fire">夢善笑恐畏</span>；<span class="c-earth">脾氣盛</span>，則<span class="c-earth">夢歌、身體重不舉</span>；<span class="c-water">腎氣盛</span>，則<span class="c-water">夢腰脊兩解不屬</span>。凡此十二盛者，至而瀉之，立已。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜十五不足・厥氣客處夢象]</span>
<div class="model-map">厥氣客於<span class="c-fire">心</span>，則<span class="c-fire">夢見丘山煙火</span>；客於<span class="c-metal">肺</span>，則<span class="c-metal">夢飛揚，見金鐵之奇物</span>；客於<span class="c-wood">肝</span>，則<span class="c-wood">夢山林樹木</span>；客於<span class="c-earth">脾</span>，則<span class="c-earth">夢見丘陵大澤，壞屋風雨</span>；客於<span class="c-water">腎</span>，則<span class="c-water">夢臨淵，沒居水中</span>；客於<span class="c-water">膀胱</span>，則<span class="c-water">夢遊行</span>；客於<span class="c-earth">胃</span>，則<span class="c-earth">夢飲食</span>；客於<span class="c-metal">大腸</span>，則<span class="c-metal">夢田野</span>；客於<span class="c-fire">小腸</span>，則<span class="c-fire">夢聚邑衝衢</span>；客於<span class="c-wood">膽</span>，則<span class="c-wood">夢鬥訟自刳</span>；客於<span class="c-yin">陰器</span>，則夢接內；客於項，則夢斬首；客於脛，則夢行走而不能前，及居深地窌苑中；客於股肱，則夢禮節拜起；客於胞?，則夢溲便。凡此十五不足者，至而補之立已也。</div>
