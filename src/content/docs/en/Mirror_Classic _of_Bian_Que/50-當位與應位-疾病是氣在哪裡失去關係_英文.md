One of the most difficult but important concepts in “Kuidu” is the distinction between the “proper position” and the “response position.”

The text says:

> What overcomes occupies its proper position and corresponds with the tone of its governing She; recovery responds at its position and corresponds with the lü of its governing She. What occupies its proper position is the She of qi interaction; what responds at its position is where qi travels into meeting.

Later it states even more clearly:

> The proper position is the origin of change; the response position is the transformation of disease.

## Proper position: where change originates

The text calls the infusion and meeting of channel-passage qi “qi of the proper position.” It is the original qi interaction occurring at a particular joint-meeting site.

The proper position is therefore closer to **the source position of change**.

## Response position: where change becomes visible pathological transformation

The response position is linked with relations such as Zhuan, Sheng, Jian, and Bing and is the position affected after qi has moved onward.

The response position is therefore closer to **the landing point of reaction or pathological transformation**.

## Why does this distinction matter?

Because the location where symptoms are observed is not necessarily the location where the initial change arose.

One site may be the source and another the response. If the origin is inferred directly from where pain or discomfort appears, these two levels can easily be collapsed.

This is entirely consistent with the cross-channel pairings of the Eight She: qi interaction can cross left-right, upper-lower, and hand-foot channel relationships, so disease can appear at another end of the relational chain.

## Kuidu attempts to reconnect source and response positions

The text concludes:

> Diagnose the communicating measures of the three Guan and the Hou of the proper position at the Qi Mouth; infer the measure of qi transformation and establish the constants of its shifts and entry-exit. By hearing sound and palpating pulse, one thereby knows the measure of the meeting of the three qi, reaches the location of the She, and knows where disease is diseased.

In other words, the process begins from what is observed at the Qi-Mouth Hou and infers the deeper qi interaction and response position.
