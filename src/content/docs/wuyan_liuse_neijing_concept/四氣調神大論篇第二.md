<span class="model-tag">[四象－時間／年尺度｜春夏秋冬]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜四時應五臟]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yinyang">春</span>三月，<span class="c-yinyang">此為發陳</span>。天地俱<span class="c-yinyang">生</span>，萬物以榮，<span class="c-yinyang">夜臥早起</span>，廣步於庭，被發緩形，以<span class="c-yinyang">使志生</span>，<span class="c-yinyang">生</span>而勿殺，予而勿奪，賞而勿罰，此<span class="c-yinyang">春</span>氣之應，養<span class="c-yinyang">生</span>之道也；逆之則傷<span class="c-wood">肝</span>，<span class="c-yangyang">夏</span>為<span class="c-water">實寒變</span>，奉<span class="c-yangyang">長</span>者少。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yangyang">夏</span>三月，<span class="c-yangyang">此為蕃秀</span>。天地氣交，萬物華實，<span class="c-yangyang">夜臥早起</span>，無厭於日，<span class="c-yangyang">使志勿怒</span>，使華英成秀，使氣得洩，若所愛在外，此<span class="c-yangyang">夏</span>氣之應，養<span class="c-yangyang">長</span>之道也；逆之則傷<span class="c-fire">心</span>，<span class="c-yangyin">秋</span>為<span class="c-shaoyang">痎瘧</span>，奉<span class="c-yangyin">收</span>者少，<span class="c-yinyin">冬</span>至重病。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yangyin">秋</span>三月，<span class="c-yangyin">此謂容平</span>。天氣以急，地氣以明，<span class="c-yangyin">早臥早起</span>，與雞俱興，<span class="c-yangyin">使志安寧</span>，以緩<span class="c-yangyin">秋</span>刑，<span class="c-yangyin">收</span>斂神氣，使<span class="c-yangyin">秋</span>氣平，無外其志，使<span class="c-metal">肺</span>氣清，此<span class="c-yangyin">秋</span>氣之應，養<span class="c-yangyin">收</span>之道也；逆之則傷<span class="c-metal">肺</span>，<span class="c-yinyin">冬</span>為<span class="c-earth">飧洩</span>，奉<span class="c-yinyin">藏</span>者少。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yinyin">冬</span>三月，<span class="c-yinyin">此為閉藏</span>。水冰地坼，勿擾乎<span class="c-yang">陽</span>，<span class="c-yinyin">早臥晚起</span>，必待日光，<span class="c-yinyin">使志若伏若匿，若有私意，若已有得</span>，去<span class="c-cold">寒</span>就<span class="c-warm">溫</span>，無洩皮膚，使氣極奪。此<span class="c-yinyin">冬</span>氣之應，養<span class="c-yinyin">藏</span>之道也；逆之則傷<span class="c-water">腎</span>，<span class="c-yinyang">春</span>為<span class="c-earth">痿厥</span>，奉<span class="c-yinyang">生</span>者少。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／天地｜天地交通]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yang">天氣</span>清淨，光明者也，藏德不止，故不下也。</div>
<div class="model-map">天明則日月不明，邪害空竅。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>者閉塞，<span class="c-yin">地氣</span>者冒明，雲霧不精，則上應白露不下。</div>
<div class="model-map">交通不表，萬物命故不施，不施則名木多死。</div>
<div class="model-map">惡氣不發，風雨不節，白露不下，則菀不榮。</div>
<div class="model-map">賊風數至，暴雨數起，天地四時不相保，與道相失，則未央絕滅。</div>
<div class="model-map">唯聖人從之，故身無奇病，萬物不失，生氣不竭。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[四象－時間／年尺度｜逆四時]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體]</span>
<div class="model-map">逆<span class="c-yinyang">春</span>氣則<span class="c-yinyang">少陽</span>不<span class="c-yinyang">生</span>，<span class="c-wood">肝</span>氣內變。</div>
<div class="model-map">逆<span class="c-yangyang">夏</span>氣則<span class="c-yangyang">太陽</span>不<span class="c-yangyang">長</span>，<span class="c-fire">心</span>氣內洞。</div>
<div class="model-map">逆<span class="c-yangyin">秋</span>氣則<span class="c-yangyin">太陰</span>不<span class="c-yangyin">收</span>，<span class="c-metal">肺</span>氣焦滿。</div>
<div class="model-map">逆<span class="c-yinyin">冬</span>氣則<span class="c-yinyin">少陰</span>不<span class="c-yinyin">藏</span>，<span class="c-water">腎</span>氣獨沈。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－時間／年尺度｜春夏養陽・秋冬養陰]</span>
<div class="model-map">夫四時<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>者，萬物之根本也。所以聖人<span class="c-yinyang">春</span><span class="c-yangyang">夏</span>養<span class="c-yang">陽</span>，<span class="c-yangyin">秋</span><span class="c-yinyin">冬</span>養<span class="c-yin">陰</span>，以從其根；故與萬物沈浮於生長之門。逆其根則伐其本，壞其真矣。</div>
<div class="model-map">故<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>四時者，萬物之終始也；生死之本也；逆之則災害生，從之則苛疾不起，是謂得道。</div>
<div class="model-map">道者，聖人行之，愚者佩之。從<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>則生，逆之則死；從之則治，逆之則亂。反順為逆，是謂內格。</div>
是故聖人不治已病，治未病；不治已亂，治未亂，此之謂也。夫病已成而後藥之，亂己成而後治之，譬猶渴而穿井，鬥而鑄錐，不亦晚乎？
