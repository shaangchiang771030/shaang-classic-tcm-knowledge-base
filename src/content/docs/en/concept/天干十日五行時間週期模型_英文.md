The Five-Phase model can not only be expanded to the macroscopic annual scale of 360 days, with each phase governing 72 days; it can also be compressed to a microscopic cycle measured in days. This is the **ten-day Five-Phase cycle of the Heavenly Stems**. It is a framework in which the ten Heavenly Stem symbols are used to capture finer fluctuations of Five-Phase energy in time.

The core first-principles idea is the **partitioning and encoding of a decimal celestial resonance cycle**. The ten Heavenly Stems—Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, and Gui—are grouped in adjacent Yin-Yang pairs to form five sets: Jia-Yi as Wood, Bing-Ding as Fire, Wu-Ji as Earth, Geng-Xin as Metal, and Ren-Gui as Water. Each pair governs two days, completing a Five-Phase microcycle within ten days. This ten-day cycle becomes an indispensable parameter in traditional temporal calculations.
One must be careful, however: although Jia and Yi both belong to Wood, their Yin-Yang qualities differ. In the musical theory associated here with ancient Chinese medicine, Jia and Yi represent different high and low frequency states within the same *jue* tone. The acupuncture method known for centuries as **ziwu liuzhu** (“midnight-noon ebb and flow”) likewise embeds the order in which points open according to stems and branches within this ten-day cycle.
