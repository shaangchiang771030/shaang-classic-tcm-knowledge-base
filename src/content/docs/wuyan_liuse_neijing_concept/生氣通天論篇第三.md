黃帝曰：夫自古通天者，生之本，本於<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>。  
天地之間，六合之內，其氣<span class="model-tag">[九宮（八風）－空間]</span>九州、九竅、五臟十二節，皆通乎天氣。  
其生五，其氣三，數犯此者，則邪氣傷人，此壽命之本也。  
蒼天之氣，清靜則志意治，順之則<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>固，雖有賊邪，弗能害也，此因時之序。  
<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體｜內外衛固]</span>
<div class="model-map">故聖人傳精神，服天氣而通神明。失之則<span class="c-yin">內</span>閉九竅，<span class="c-yang">外</span>壅肌肉，衛氣解散，此謂自傷，氣之削也。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>者，若天與日，失其所，則折壽而不彰。故天運當以日光明。是故<span class="c-yang">陽因而上</span>，<span class="c-yang">衛外</span>者也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[六淫－空間｜寒暑濕傷陽]</span>
<div class="model-map">因於<span class="c-cold">寒</span>，<span class="c-cold">欲如運樞，起居如驚，神氣乃浮</span>。</div>
<div class="model-map">因於<span class="c-hot">暑</span>，<span class="c-hot">汗，煩則喘喝，靜則多言，體若燔炭，汗出而散</span>。</div>
<div class="model-map">因於<span class="c-earth">濕</span>，<span class="c-earth">首如裹，濕熱不攘，大筋緛短，小筋馳長。緛短為拘，馳長為痿</span>。</div>
<div class="model-map">因於氣，為腫，四維相代，<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>乃竭。</div>
<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>者，<span class="c-yang">煩勞則張，精絕，辟積於夏，使人煎厥；目盲不可以視，耳閉不可以聽，潰潰乎若壞都，汨汨乎不可止</span>。  
<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>者，<span class="c-yang">大怒則形氣絕而血菀於上，使人薄厥</span>。  
有傷於筋，縱，其若不容。  
汗出偏沮，使人偏枯。  
汗出見<span class="c-earth">濕</span>，乃生痤疿。  
高梁之變，足生大丁，受如持虛。  
勞汗當<span class="c-wood">風</span>，<span class="c-cold">寒</span>薄為皶，郁乃痤。  
<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>者，精則養神，柔則養筋。  
開闔不得，<span class="c-cold">寒</span>氣從之，乃生大僂。  
陷脈為瘻，留連肉腠，俞氣化薄，傳為<span class="c-wood">善畏，及為驚駭</span>。  
營氣不從，逆於肉理，乃生癰腫。  
魄汗未盡，形弱而氣爍，穴俞以閉，發為<span class="c-wood">風</span>瘧。  
故<span class="c-wood">風</span>者，百病之始也，清靜則肉腠閉拒，雖有大<span class="c-wood">風</span>苛毒，弗之能害，此因時之序也。  
故病久則傳化，<span class="c-yang">上</span><span class="c-yin">下</span>不並，良醫弗為。  
故陽畜積病死，而<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>當隔。隔者當瀉，不亟正治，粗乃敗之。  

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－時間／日尺度｜陽氣一日盛衰]</span>
<div class="model-map">故<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>者，一日而主<span class="c-yang">外</span>。平旦人氣生，日中而<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>隆，日西而<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>已虛，氣門乃閉。</div>
<div class="model-map">是故<span class="c-yin">暮</span>而<span class="c-yin">收拒</span>，<span class="c-yin">無擾筋骨</span>，<span class="c-yin">無見霧露</span>，反此三時，形乃困薄。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／人體｜藏精・衛外・陰陽平衡]</span>
<div class="model-map">岐伯曰：<span class="c-yin">陰</span>者，藏精而起亟也，<span class="c-yang">陽</span>者，衛<span class="c-yang">外</span>而為固也。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yin">陰</span>不勝其<span class="c-yang">陽</span>，則脈流薄疾，並乃狂。<span class="c-yang">陽</span>不勝其<span class="c-yin">陰</span>，則五臟氣爭，九竅不通。</div>
<div class="model-map">是以聖人陳<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>，筋脈和同，骨髓堅固，氣血皆從。如是則<span class="c-yin">內</span><span class="c-yang">外</span>調和，邪不能害，耳目聰明，氣立如故。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-wood">風</span>客淫氣，精乃亡，邪傷<span class="c-wood">肝</span>也。</div>
<div class="model-map">因而飽食，筋脈橫解，腸澼為痔。</div>
<div class="model-map">因而大飲，則氣逆。</div>
<div class="model-map">因而強力，<span class="c-water">腎</span>氣乃傷，高骨乃壞。</div>
<div class="model-map">凡<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>之要，<span class="c-yang">陽</span>密乃固，兩者不和，若春無秋，若冬無夏。因而和之，是謂聖度。</div>
<div class="model-map">故<span class="c-yang">陽</span>強不能密，<span class="c-yin">陰</span>氣乃絕。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yin">陰</span>平<span class="c-yang">陽</span>秘，精神乃治；<span class="c-yin">陰</span><span class="c-yang">陽</span>離決，精氣乃絕。</div>
因於露<span class="c-wood">風</span>，乃生<span class="c-cold">寒</span><span class="c-hot">熱</span>。  

<span class="model-tag">[四象－時間／年尺度｜四時受邪]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[六淫－年尺度]</span>
<div class="model-map">是以<span class="c-yinyang">春</span>傷於<span class="c-wood">風</span>，<span class="c-earth">邪氣留連</span>，乃為<span class="c-earth">洞洩</span>。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yangyang">夏</span>傷於<span class="c-hot">暑</span>，<span class="c-yangyin">秋</span>為<span class="c-shaoyang">痎瘧</span>。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yangyin">秋</span>傷於<span class="c-earth">濕</span>，<span class="c-metal">上逆而咳</span>，發為<span class="c-earth">痿厥</span>。</div>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yinyin">冬</span>傷於<span class="c-cold">寒</span>，<span class="c-yinyang">春</span>必<span class="c-fire">溫病</span>。</div>
<div class="model-map">四時之氣，更傷五臟。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[五行－空間／人體｜五味偏勝]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-yin">陰</span>之所生，本在五味；<span class="c-yin">陰</span>之五宮，傷在五味。</div>
<div class="model-map">是故味過於<span class="c-wood">酸</span>，<span class="c-wood">肝氣</span>以津，<span class="c-earth">脾氣</span>乃絕。</div>
<div class="model-map">味過於<span class="c-water">咸</span>，<span class="c-water">大骨</span>氣勞，短肌，<span class="c-fire">心氣</span>抑。</div>
<div class="model-map">味過於<span class="c-earth">甘</span>，<span class="c-fire">心氣</span>喘滿，色<span class="c-water">黑</span>，<span class="c-water">腎氣</span>不衡。</div>
<div class="model-map">味過於<span class="c-fire">苦</span>，<span class="c-earth">脾氣</span>不濡，<span class="c-earth">胃氣</span>乃厚。</div>
<div class="model-map">味過於<span class="c-metal">辛</span>，<span class="c-wood">筋脈</span>沮弛，精神乃央。</div>
是故謹和五味，骨正筋柔，氣血以流，腠理以密，如是則骨氣以精。謹道如法，長有天命。
