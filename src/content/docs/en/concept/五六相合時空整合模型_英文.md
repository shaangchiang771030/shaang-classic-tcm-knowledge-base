After passing through the orthogonal foundational models of Yin-Yang, the Four Images, the Five Phases, and the Three Yin and Three Yang, we reach a major point of convergence in the study of Heaven and humanity: the **Five-Six spatiotemporal integration model**.
“Combining five and six” means that across both temporal and spatial dimensions, the ancients brought together the **system of five**—the Five Phases and Heavenly Stems—and the **system of six**—the Three Yin and Three Yang and Earthly Branches—in a matrix-like interaction, forming two great symbolic gears that continue to rotate together.
Mathematically, the systems of five and six are coprime and independent.
Three examples are proposed. **First**, when they combine in time, they generate the **sixty Jiazi**, the sexagenary cycle that forms a backbone of the traditional Chinese calendar. The number sixty appears on both sides of this spatiotemporal mapping, producing an especially symmetrical structure.
**Second**, when the two systems combine in the spatial organization of the human body, they generate the **Five-Shu point system** of the channels: six channel categories of the Three Yin and Three Yang × five Five-Phase Shu points on each channel = **sixty core Shu points**.
