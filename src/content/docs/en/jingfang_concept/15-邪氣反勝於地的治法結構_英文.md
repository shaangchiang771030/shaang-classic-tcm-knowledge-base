The “Great Treatise on the Utmost Truth” passages on **pathogenic qi counter-victory** appear to describe how each of the Six Qi should be treated when another pathogenic qi overcomes it in reverse. Formal logical testing, however, yields a more important conclusion: the first therapeutic flavor is not determined by the counter-victorious pathogenic qi alone. It is determined by the **resident root qi whose proper position has been counter-overcome**.

  <figcaption>When a qi governs on Earth and is counter-overcome by pathogenic qi, the first therapeutic flavor falls on the resident root qi that has been counter-overcome, while thermal nature opposes the pathogenic qi that gained the advantage.</figcaption>
</figure>

Thus, when “Wind governs on Earth and clear/dry qi counter-victoriously overcomes it,” the qi that has actually gained the advantage is clear/dry Metal, yet the first therapeutic flavor is not calculated from how to overcome that clear pathogen. Instead, it takes the draining flavor of Wind-Wood, the resident root qi, and therefore uses sour-warm. By the same logic, when Heat or Fire governs on Earth and Cold counter-overcomes it, the first flavor is the Fire draining flavor sweet; when Dampness governs on Earth and Heat counter-overcomes it, the first flavor is the Earth draining flavor bitter; and when Cold governs on Earth and Heat counter-overcomes it, the first flavor is the Water draining flavor salty. In the identifiable Earth-governing counter-victory passages, this rule reproduces 5/5; when the Heaven-governing counter-victory passages are included, it reproduces 11/11.

This means that treatment of “pathogenic qi counter-victory” should not be understood as “see the pathogen, then choose a flavor that overcomes it.” Programmatic testing shows that choosing the first flavor solely from the identity of the counter-victorious pathogen hits only 3/6. The reason is that the same Heat pathogen, when counter-overcoming different root qi, can be met in the text by bitter, pungent, or salty as the first flavor. The first flavor is therefore not a simple function of pathogen identity, but is located at the qi-transformation interface where **the resident root qi has been counter-overcome**.

The most concise formulation is:

**Pathogenic qi counter-victory on Earth:**
resident root qi is counter-overcome
→ first therapeutic flavor = draining flavor of the resident root qi
→ thermal nature then counteracts the pathogenic qi that actually gained the advantage

For example, when the Heat or Fire root qi is counter-overcome by Cold, sweet is used first because sweet is the draining flavor of Fire, while a hot thermal nature is used to counter Cold. When the Cold root qi is counter-overcome by Heat, salty is used first because salty is the draining flavor of Water, while a cool/cold thermal nature counters Heat. The first flavor and the thermal property therefore belong to two different layers: **flavor addresses imbalance of the root qi; thermal nature addresses the counter-victorious pathogenic qi.**

A special case is the line, “When Dryness governs on Earth and Heat counter-victoriously overcomes it, treat with *ping* and cold.” The word *ping* here is not one of the five flavors—sour, bitter, sweet, pungent, salty—and therefore cannot be treated as normal first-flavor data. The formal model predicts that the most likely missing first flavor is **pungent**, because pungent is the draining flavor of Metal and the parallel Sitian passage explicitly says, “When Dryness transforms in Heaven and Heat counter-victoriously overcomes it, treat with pungent and cold.” Textually, however, the original wording *ping han* must still be preserved as a missing value or possible textual corruption; a model-based reconstruction cannot be passed off as the original wording.

The true value of this “pathogenic qi counter-victory” group therefore lies not in memorizing six flavor prescriptions, but in seeing the structural layers of *Inner Canon* treatment: counter-victory does not mean merely treating the pathogen; it first stabilizes the resident root qi that has been counter-overcome. The first flavor is the root qi’s draining flavor, while thermal nature counters the counter-victorious pathogen. This comes closer than a line-by-line table to a computable grammar of qi-transformation treatment and illustrates the highly formalizable internal order of the flavor-treatment system in the “Great Treatise on the Utmost Truth.”
