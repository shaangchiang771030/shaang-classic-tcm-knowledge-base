<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－時間／年尺度｜春夏陽多・秋冬陰多]</span>
<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－空間／天地｜天地上下]</span>
<div class="model-map">岐伯曰：<span class="c-yang">天</span><span class="c-yin">地</span>相感，<span class="c-cold">寒</span><span class="c-hot">暖</span>相移，陰陽之道，孰少孰多，<span class="c-yin">陰道</span>偶，<span class="c-yang">陽道</span>奇。發於<span class="c-wood">春</span><span class="c-fire">夏</span>，<span class="c-yin">陰氣</span>少，<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>多，陰陽不調，何補何瀉。發於<span class="c-metal">秋</span><span class="c-water">冬</span>，<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>少，<span class="c-yin">陰氣</span>多；<span class="c-yin">陰氣</span>盛而<span class="c-yang">陽氣</span>衰，故莖葉枯槁，<span class="c-earth">濕</span>雨<span class="c-yin">下</span>歸，陰陽相移，何瀉何補。奇邪離經，不可勝數，不知根結，<span class="c-yin">五臟</span><span class="c-yang">六腑</span>，折關敗樞，開合而走，陰陽大失，不可復取。九針之玄，要在終始；故能知終始，一言而畢，不知終始，針道咸絕。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－空間／人體｜三陽根結・開合樞]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-taiyang">太陽</span>根於<span class="c-taiyang">至陰</span>，結於<span class="c-taiyang">命門</span>。<span class="c-taiyang">命門</span>者，<span class="c-taiyang">目</span>也。<span class="c-yangming">陽明</span>根於<span class="c-yangming">厲兌</span>，結於<span class="c-yangming">顙大</span>。<span class="c-yangming">顙大</span>者，<span class="c-yangming">鉗耳</span>也。<span class="c-shaoyang">少陽</span>根於<span class="c-shaoyang">竅陰</span>，結於<span class="c-shaoyang">窗籠</span>。<span class="c-shaoyang">窗籠</span>者，<span class="c-shaoyang">耳中</span>也。<span class="c-taiyang">太陽</span>為<span class="c-taiyang">開</span>，<span class="c-yangming">陽明</span>為<span class="c-yangming">合</span>，<span class="c-shaoyang">少陽</span>為<span class="c-shaoyang">樞</span>，故<span class="c-taiyang">開</span>折，則<span class="c-taiyang">肉節瀆而暴病起</span>矣。故<span class="c-taiyang">暴病</span>者，取之<span class="c-taiyang">太陽</span>，視有餘不足。瀆者，皮肉宛膲而弱也。<span class="c-yangming">合</span>折，則<span class="c-yangming">氣無所止息而痿疾起</span>矣。故<span class="c-yangming">痿疾</span>者，取之<span class="c-yangming">陽明</span>，視有餘不足。無所止息者，真氣稽留，邪氣居之也。<span class="c-shaoyang">樞</span>折，即<span class="c-shaoyang">骨繇而不安於地</span>。故<span class="c-shaoyang">骨繇</span>者，取之<span class="c-shaoyang">少陽</span>，視有餘不足。<span class="c-shaoyang">骨繇</span>者，<span class="c-shaoyang">節緩而不收</span>也。所謂<span class="c-shaoyang">骨繇</span>者，搖故也。當竊其本也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－空間／人體｜三陰根結・病候]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>根於<span class="c-taiyin">隱白</span>，結於<span class="c-taiyin">太倉</span>。<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>根於<span class="c-shaoyin">湧泉</span>，結於<span class="c-shaoyin">廉泉</span>。<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>根於<span class="c-jueyin">大敦</span>，結於<span class="c-jueyin">玉英</span>，絡於<span class="c-jueyin">羶中</span>。<span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>為合，<span class="c-shaoyang">少陽</span>為樞。故開折，則<span class="c-taiyin">倉廩無所輸，膈洞</span>。<span class="c-taiyin">膈洞</span>者，取之<span class="c-taiyin">太陰</span>，視有餘不足，故開折者，氣不足而生病也。合折，即<span class="c-jueyin">氣絕而喜悲</span>。悲者取之<span class="c-jueyin">厥陰</span>，視有餘不足。樞折，則<span class="c-shaoyin">脈有所結而不通</span>。<span class="c-shaoyin">不通</span>者，取之<span class="c-shaoyin">少陰</span>，視有餘不足，有結者，皆取之不足。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[三陰三陽－空間／人體｜六陽根溜注入]</span>
<div class="model-map"><span class="c-taiyang">足太陽</span>根於<span class="c-taiyang">至陰</span>，溜於<span class="c-taiyang">京骨</span>，注於<span class="c-taiyang">崑崙</span>，入於<span class="c-taiyang">天柱</span>、<span class="c-taiyang">飛揚</span>也。<span class="c-shaoyang">足少陽</span>根於<span class="c-shaoyang">竅陰</span>，溜於<span class="c-shaoyang">丘墟</span>，注於<span class="c-shaoyang">陽輔</span>，入於<span class="c-shaoyang">天容</span>、<span class="c-shaoyang">光明</span>也。<span class="c-yangming">足陽明</span>根於<span class="c-yangming">厲兌</span>，溜於<span class="c-yangming">衝陽</span>，注於<span class="c-yangming">下陵</span>，入於<span class="c-yangming">人迎</span>，<span class="c-yangming">豐隆</span>也。<span class="c-taiyang">手太陽</span>根於<span class="c-taiyang">少澤</span>，溜於<span class="c-taiyang">陽谷</span>，注於<span class="c-taiyang">小海</span>，入於<span class="c-taiyang">天窗</span>，<span class="c-taiyang">支正</span>也。<span class="c-shaoyang">少陽</span>根於<span class="c-shaoyang">關衝</span>，溜於<span class="c-shaoyang">陽池</span>，注於<span class="c-shaoyang">支溝</span>，入於<span class="c-shaoyang">天牖</span>、<span class="c-shaoyang">外關</span>也。<span class="c-yangming">手陽明</span>根於<span class="c-yangming">商陽</span>，溜於<span class="c-yangming">合谷</span>，注於<span class="c-yangming">陽溪</span>，入於<span class="c-yangming">扶突</span>、<span class="c-yangming">偏歷</span>也。此所謂十二經者，盛絡皆當取之。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－時間／日尺度｜一日一夜五十營]</span>
<div class="model-map">一<span class="c-yang">日</span>一<span class="c-yin">夜</span>五十營，以營<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>之精，不應數者，名曰狂生。所謂五十營者，<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆受氣，持其脈口，數其至也。五十動而不一代者，<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>皆受氣。四十動一代者，一臟無氣。三十動一代者，二臟無氣。二十動一代者，三臟無氣。十動一代者，四臟無氣。不滿十動一代者，<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>無氣。予之短期，要在終始。所謂五十動而不一代者，以為常也。以知<span class="c-yin">五臟</span>之期，予之短期者，乍數乍疏也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－刺法｜深淺疾徐]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：逆順五體者，言人骨節之大小，肉之堅脆，皮之厚薄，血之清濁，氣之滑澀，脈之長短，血之多少，經絡之數，余已知之矣，此皆布衣匹夫之士也。夫王公大人，血食之君，身體柔脆，肌肉軟弱，血氣慓悍滑利，其刺之徐<span class="c-yang">疾</span><span class="c-yang">淺</span><span class="c-yin">深</span>多少，可得同之乎。岐伯答曰：膏梁菽藿之味，何可同也？氣滑即出<span class="c-yang">疾</span>，其氣澀則出<span class="c-yin">遲</span>，氣悍則針小而入<span class="c-yang">淺</span>，氣澀則針大而入<span class="c-yin">深</span>，<span class="c-yin">深</span>則欲<span class="c-yin">留</span>，<span class="c-yang">淺</span>則欲<span class="c-yang">疾</span>。以此觀之，刺布衣者，<span class="c-yin">深</span>以<span class="c-yin">留</span>之，刺大人者，微以徐之，此皆因氣慓悍滑利也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－刺法｜形氣有餘不足]</span>
<div class="model-map">黃帝曰：形氣之逆順奈何？岐伯曰：形氣不足，病氣有餘，是邪勝也，急瀉之；形氣有餘，病氣不足，急補之；形氣不足，病氣不足，此<span class="c-yin">陰陽氣俱不足</span>也，不可刺之，刺之則重不足。重不足則<span class="c-yin">陰陽俱竭</span>，血氣皆盡，五臟空虛，筋骨髓枯，老者絕滅，壯者不復矣。形氣有餘，病氣有餘，此謂<span class="c-yang">陰陽俱有餘</span>也。急瀉其邪，調其虛實。故曰：有餘者瀉之，不足者補之，此之謂也。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－刺法｜補瀉逆順]</span>
<div class="model-map">故曰：刺不知逆順，真邪相搏。滿而補之，則<span class="c-yang">陰陽四溢</span>，腸胃充郭，肝肺內（月真），<span class="c-neutral">陰陽相錯</span>。虛而瀉之，則經脈空虛，血氣竭枯，腸胃（亻耳耳耳）辟，皮膚薄著，毛腠夭膲，予之死期。</div>

<span class="model-tag">[陰陽－刺法｜調陰與陽]</span>
<div class="model-map">故曰：用針之要，在於知調<span class="c-yin">陰</span>與<span class="c-yang">陽</span>。調<span class="c-yin">陰</span>與<span class="c-yang">陽</span>，精氣乃光，合形與氣，使神內藏。故曰：上工平氣，中工亂脈，下工絕氣危生。故曰：下工不可不慎也，必審五藏變化之病，五脈之應，經絡之實虛，皮之柔麤，而後取之也。</div>
