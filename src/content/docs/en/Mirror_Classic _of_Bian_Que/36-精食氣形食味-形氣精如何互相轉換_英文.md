The second part of “Tongtian” gives an extremely important generative model in four short phrases:

> Essence feeds on qi; form feeds on taste; transformation generates essence; qi generates form.

If read only through a modern matter/energy dichotomy, these four lines are easily oversimplified. The *Mirror Classic* is actually describing a **cycle of mutual transformation**.

## Form is not maintained by form alone

“Form feeds on taste” means that the tangible body depends on nourishment from food and drink. But the text does not stop there; it also says “qi generates form.”

This means maintenance and generation of bodily form depend not only on material intake but also on processes of qi transformation. After the Stomach and Intestines receive and contain, they must “transform substances and change them into qi,” and qi then participates in the generation of form and essence.

In this model, food entering the body therefore does not directly equal muscle, blood, or bone; it must pass through transformation.

## Essence is not a fixed substance sealed inside the body

The phrases “essence feeds on qi” and “transformation generates essence” indicate that essence too is continuously nourished and transformed by qi.

A later passage says:

> Essence is true qi, the root of the body. True qi is the clear, endowed, and grain qi moving through the body in order to nourish the qi of the Five Zang.

Here clear qi, endowed qi, and grain qi are placed together as sources of true qi. Essence and true qi are therefore not stores isolated from the outside; they are continuously sustained by congenital and acquired inputs.

## “Transformation” is the key middle term

If drawn as the simplest arrow sequence:

**taste → form → qi → essence → transformation → generation of new form, qi, and essence.**

Yet the source is not a one-way assembly line. It repeatedly uses verbs such as “return,” “store,” “generate,” “move,” “grow,” and “nurture.” A better understanding is therefore that form, qi, and essence constitute a cyclic generative network.

## This also explains why disease cannot be read from form alone

If a local tissue develops a problem, the *Mirror Classic* would not ask only “what structure is damaged?” It would also ask where the qi supporting that structure comes from, how essence is infused, whether channel passages are open, and whether Hou and She have lost their proper positions.

This is why “Tongtian” reconnects Heavenly and Earthly inputs with the zang-fu.
