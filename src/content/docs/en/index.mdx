## Why Cosmos Order Exists

Classical medicine left behind many bodies of language that can appear disconnected: yin and yang, the Five Phases, the Three Yin and Three Yang, the Six Qi, meridians, the Five Flavors, pulse, formulas, and seasonal timing. Cosmos Order does not aim to repeat readily available general knowledge. It returns to primary texts and structural relationships to ask whether these systems share deeper patterns that can be repeatedly examined.

We treat the proposition that celestial cycles and circular motion may underlie multiple classical models as a **research thesis**, not as an established scientific fact. Textual evidence, modeling, counterexamples, and data will determine how far the hypothesis can go.

## How Cosmos Order Works

**Observation → abstraction → modeling → cross-validation → counterexamples → application.**

The goal is not only to remember conclusions, but to understand how classical knowledge is derived, where uncertainty remains, and how a model can return to real bodies and real life.
