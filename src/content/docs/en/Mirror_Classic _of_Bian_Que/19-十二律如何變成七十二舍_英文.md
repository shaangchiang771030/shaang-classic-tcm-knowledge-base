After pairing the Twelve Lü with the Six Channels, the *Mirror Classic of Bian Que* does not add more channel names. Instead it says:

> Qi manifestations transform into lü as ascent, descent, entry, and exit. Through the odd-even Twelve Lü, the qi of the Seventy-Two She proceeds. The Seventy-Two She are the entry and exit of constant qi as transformation changes.

Here the Twelve Lü are formally connected to the “Seventy-Two She.”

## Lü describes movement, not stillness

The text summarizes the Twelve Lü as “ascent, descent, entry, and exit.”

Their medical function is therefore not simply to label a channel but to describe the regulated measure of qi in motion. When the Twelve Lü combine with other qi-transformational dimensions, this movement can be placed in different She.

She should first be understood as a **state-position at which qi transformation and qi interaction come to reside**, not as a simple synonym for an acupoint.

## Why seventy-two?

The text supplies its own generating logic through the “odd-even Twelve Lü” and later connects the Seventy-Two She to the Sixty Shou, Nine Qi, Hou, and other structures.

At this stage there is no need to force seventy-two into one unique multiplication formula. The more important point is the hierarchy:

**the Twelve Lü are a coarser scale of movement; the Seventy-Two She form a finer space of qi-transformational positions.**

The author later says, “The Sixty Shou are the beginning of the divisions of She.” The Sixty Shou are therefore the point of entry into the finer She structure.

## The word “She” is unusually apt

She can mean a lodging or a place of temporary residence.

The *Mirror Classic* has already said that “where qi resides is where Ying and Wei lodge,” and that She are associated with meetings of streams and valleys. Qi is not permanently fixed there; during flow and infusion it temporarily forms an interaction state at a junction.

She therefore carries both **location** and **temporary state occupancy**.

## The Seventy-Two She provide a container for “shallowness and depth of disease location”

If the Sixty Shou are to measure disease depth, there must be a space in which “disease location” can be represented. The Seventy-Two She supply such a space.

For now the roles can be separated as:

- the ten transformation tones describe the relation of qi;
- the Twelve Lü describe how qi moves;
- the Sixty Shou combine the two into a starting code;
- the Seventy-Two She describe where that qi-transformational state resides in the interaction network.
